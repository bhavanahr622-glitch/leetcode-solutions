# Two Sum

## Problem
Given an array of integers and a target value, find the indices of two numbers whose sum equals the target.

## Difficulty
Easy

## LeetCode
Two Sum

## Approach
I used two nested loops to check every possible pair of elements.
When the sum of two elements equals the target, their indices are stored and returned.

## Complexity
- Time: O(n²)
- Space: O(1)
